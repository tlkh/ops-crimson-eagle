# Generated UI assets — 9 October 2026

These are original assets created for the game with the built-in ImageGen mode. They are not photographs of the incident areas or documentary evidence for either campaign. The card scenes are illustrative; the simulation's maps and aircraft geometry remain separately authored.

The user rejected an earlier detailed aircraft emblem because its Chinook drawing was distorted. It is not shipped. The final mark uses only broad crimson wing shapes, two short green rotor bars and one gold water droplet. The app icon places that same spare mark on a dark pine field, with the mark within the central maskable safe area and no rounded-corner treatment.

## Final ImageGen prompt set

1. **Transparent mark:** “Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: very simple transparent game logo symbol for Operation Crimson Eagle. Make a bold abstract mark, not an illustration: one broad crimson V-shaped pair of eagle wings, two short parallel dark-green horizontal bars suggesting tandem helicopter rotors, and one small warm-gold water droplet centered below. No aircraft body at all. Build from only a few flat solid shapes with clean geometry and generous negative space, readable at 32 pixels, balanced square composition. It should feel like a premium minimalist game emblem, akin to a strong app glyph, with no fine detail, no texture, no gradients. Background genuinely transparent. Absolutely no Chinook drawing, no cockpit, no fuselage, no wheels, no eagle head, no feathers, no outline strokes, no text, no letters, no crest ring, no national or military insignia, no watermark.”
2. **App icon:** Preserve the final simple mark, scale it to approximately 60% of the icon square, and center it on an opaque dark pine background (`#10201d`). Keep the surrounding field generous and the mark legible at small sizes. No text, aircraft detail, border or rounded corners.
3. **Singapore card:** A wide, restrained environmental image of flat Borneo peat lowland at dawn, with forest, a lake, a meandering river and distant sea. Keep the horizon low and flat. No mountains, aircraft, text, marks or identifiable people. Natural muted colour and atmosphere suited to a serious humanitarian flight game.
4. **Japan card:** A wide, restrained environmental image of flat West Kalimantan peat swamp forest under an overcast sky, with modest smoke and a distant fire line beside water. Avoid spectacle, mountains, aircraft, text, marks and identifiable people. Keep the scene plausible as an illustrative game card.

The generated source images were resized/exported with `sips` into the project files above. The transparent mark retains alpha; the touch and manifest icons are opaque PNGs. The two wide campaign scenes were exported as compressed JPEGs for the menu.
